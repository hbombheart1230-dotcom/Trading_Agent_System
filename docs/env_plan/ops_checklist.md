# Live Trading Validation Checklist

## Pre-Market
- system starts clean
- policy injection verified

## Intraday
- scanner candidates exist
- fallback behavior correct
- route distribution normal
- strategist calls stable

## Post-Market
- reports generated
- feedback packet present
- anomalies logged

## Critical Checks
- no-trade anomalies
- overtrading signals
- missing candidates

## Rule
DO NOT PATCH DURING MARKET
