## Stop Rule

This search does not continue by:

- changing thresholds after seeing results
- renaming near-identical rules
- extending the same retrospective window
- treating gross returns as net alpha
- promoting a calibration-only success

Any future research must begin with a new business hypothesis, a new frozen
contract, and genuinely later confirmation data. It is not Batch 3 of this
search.
