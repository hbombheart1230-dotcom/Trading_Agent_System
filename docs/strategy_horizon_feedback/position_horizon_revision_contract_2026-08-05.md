# Position Horizon Revision Contract

Date: 2026-08-05

## Decision

The entry strategy frame remains immutable, but the holding horizon is now split into two fields:

- `entry_horizon`: the Commander-approved horizon stored when BUY fills; immutable provenance
- `active_horizon`: the current Commander-approved horizon used by Monitor; mutable only through Stage 3 or Stage 4

An unrelated Stage 1 or Stage 2 cycle cannot replace either the held symbol's strategy frame or its active horizon.

## Runtime Flow

```text
Stage 1 market frame
  -> Scanner selection
  -> Stage 2 selected-symbol tactics
  -> Commander policy
  -> BUY fill stores entry_horizon and active_horizon
  -> Stage 3 same-session review may revise active_horizon
  -> Stage 4 closeout review may approve an overnight active_horizon
  -> Monitor consumes active_horizon while retaining entry_horizon provenance
```

## Stage 3 Authority

Stage 3 is scheduled by the position review clock derived from the current horizon. Repeated HOLD remains supporting evidence, not the sole scheduler.

Stage 3 may:

- maintain the current horizon
- shorten `intraday` to `scalp`
- extend `scalp` to `intraday`
- revise the same-session expected hold window
- request the next review time

Stage 3 may not authorize `overnight_probe` or `1_2day_swing`. A revision requires at least medium evidence confidence and usable data quality. Hard stop, broker/data integrity, emergency, and mandatory closeout rules remain higher authority.

## Stage 4 Authority

Stage 4 is the only LLM review that can authorize overnight carry for an open position. Commander applies the per-symbol decision and Monitor still enforces deterministic PnL, trend, VWAP, liquidity, weekend, holiday, and market-risk checks.

Legacy open positions without `position_horizon_state` retain the earlier pinned-horizon behavior during migration. Every new BUY stores the new state and therefore requires explicit Stage 4 carry approval.

## Persistence

`position_horizon_state.v1` stores:

- entry and active horizon
- entry and active expected hold windows
- entry, last-review, and next-review timestamps
- last Stage 3 and Stage 4 decisions
- Stage 4 carry approval
- bounded revision history
- applied revision IDs for idempotency

Historical checkpoint extensions are measured from the actual exit. They diagnose early or late exits, but they are not an exact replay of the new Stage 3 decision because the old Stage 3 output did not contain the new revision contract.

## Current Evidence

For 2026-06-01 through 2026-08-05:

- 111 trade models were found; 100 had comparable entry and exit prices.
- post-exit coverage was 69 at +5m, 68 at +15m, 67 at +30m, 66 at +60m, and 39 at EOD.
- no stored T+1 or T+2 checkpoint was observed, so swing and overnight effectiveness cannot be concluded historically.
- live-cost actual-exit proxy: 21.0% win rate, -0.2410% average, PF 0.6706.
- comparable scalp rows improved by 0.1037 percentage points at exit+5m, but remained negative at -0.2282% average.
- comparable intraday rows changed by -0.0074 percentage points at exit+30m, providing no broad hold-extension edge.

Conclusion: the evidence rejects unconditional longer holding. Prospective validation must determine whether Stage 3 can identify the minority of positions where a horizon revision is useful before the future path is known.
